# 00 — Product Spec

> **TL;DR.** **LanguagePro AI** (`aiexam.uz`) is a production English-proficiency platform for Uzbek and global learners. Take an IELTS- or CEFR-aligned mock exam in 30–120 minutes, get an AI-graded band score with instant explanations in your language, and track progress over time. Monetised with a freemium ladder (Free → Starter → Pro → Team) billed via Click + Payme (UZ) and Stripe (international). All AI runs on Google Gemini through the OpenAI SDK.

---

## 1. Why this exists

In Uzbekistan a single official IELTS sitting costs **$255+** and books months out. Practice tests are paper-based, scoring is opaque, and feedback is generic. Foreign-language learners — students applying abroad, professionals certifying for jobs, schools doing placement — need:

1. **Cheap, fast feedback** with a defensible band score and rubric breakdown.
2. **Adaptive difficulty** so 8 minutes of testing is informative, not exhausting.
3. **Native-language explanations** (UZ / EN for v1) on what to fix.
4. **A trustworthy interface** — clean, professional, and free of cheating shortcuts when the user has skin in the game.

LanguagePro AI delivers all four with Gemini-powered grading at a fraction of the cost, and certifies progress with shareable PDF certificates and a lifetime score history.

## 3. Core value prop & promise

> "Topshiring, javob oling, darajangizni biling — 30 daqiqada. Instant AI baholash, sizning tilingizda fikr-mulohaza."

**Promises we keep**:

1. Band score within ±0.5 of a human IELTS examiner on a 30-essay calibration set (Pearson r ≥ 0.75).
2. Speaking + writing scoring in under 30 seconds end-to-end.
3. Adaptive Reading + Listening converges to SE(θ) < 0.3 in 30 questions or fewer.
4. UZ-language feedback on writing & speaking, generated by `gemini-2.5-pro`.
5. Question content is fresh, AI-generated, validated by a 3-juror Gemini panel — never copied from real IELTS.
6. PDF certificate is cryptographically signed and verifiable at `aiexam.uz/verify/<id>`.

## 4. Feature set (what's in the product)

### Phase 1 — MVP (free public beta + tariffs visible)

- Sign up / log in (email + Google OAuth)
- Pick exam: **IELTS Reading Mini**, **CEFR Quick Placement**
- Take adaptive Reading section (5–30 items)
- See band per skill + overall + UZ/EN feedback
- Free beta: 1 attempt / month
- Starter / Pro / Team tariffs visible on pricing, but checkout stays disabled until Phase 4
- Profile, history, score timeline
- Modern landing page with pricing & FAQ

### Phase 2 — Writing & Speaking

- Writing Task 1 + Task 2 with TipTap editor; AI grades 4 IELTS criteria
- Speaking Parts 1–3 with MediaRecorder upload
- Audio scored by `gemini-2.5-flash` multimodal in one call (transcript + bands + feedback)
- Listening section: TTS audio prompts via `gemini-2.5-flash-preview-tts`
- Confidence gate: low-confidence essays → human review queue (Pro/Team only)

### Phase 3 — Pro features

- Unlimited attempts (Pro)
- PDF certificate (signed, verifiable, branded)
- Lifetime history dashboard with skill trend charts
- Vocabulary explorer (CEFR-J + EVP joined)
- Study plan: "to reach 7.0 you need ___" (recommendations)

### Phase 4 — Payments & Team

- Click + Payme integration (UZS) — see [`07-payments-and-billing.md`](07-payments-and-billing.md)
- Stripe (USD/EUR) for international
- Yearly billing with 17% discount
- Team plan: bulk seats, admin dashboard, branded certs, CSV export

### Phase 5 — Scale & polish

- Real-time speaking via LiveKit + Gemini Live (replaces async upload for Pro)
- Mobile app (React Native or PWA)
- Public API (Pro+) with API keys
- Affiliate program

> See [`12-roadmap-and-phases.md`](12-roadmap-and-phases.md) for week-by-week milestones.

## 5. Non-goals (won't build)

- Question banks of real / past IELTS questions (legal risk, see [`07-payments-and-billing.md`](07-payments-and-billing.md) §7).
- Tutor marketplace.
- Generic English-as-a-second-language courses (lessons, vocabulary trainers).
- Other languages — English only for v1. Russian / German / Arabic later if metrics justify.
- On-prem or whitelabel installs (use Team plan instead).

## 7. Brand & voice

- **Name**: LanguagePro AI (long form), **LangPro** acceptable shorthand in chat/social.
- **Domain**: `aiexam.uz`. Tagline: *AI yordamida ingliz tili — 30 daqiqada darajangizni aniqlang.*
- **Personality**: precise, friendly, modern. Not gamified ("XP! streak!"), not stuffy ("Esteemed candidate").
- **Voice in copy**:
  - UZ: do'st, lekin professional. "Sen" emas, "Siz". "Kech qoldingiz" ✗ → "Vaqt qoldi" ✓.
  - EN: clear, second-person, active. "Take the test" not "Tests can be taken".
- **Logo**: wordmark `LanguagePro` with the `Pro` accented in the brand primary; small triangular monogram (`▲`) for favicons.
- **Pictographs / illustrations**: minimal line-art in brand primary; never stock photos of generic students.

## 9. Acceptance for "MVP launched"

The product is launch-ready when **all** of the following are true:

- [ ] Landing page on `aiexam.uz` passes Lighthouse Performance ≥ 90 mobile.
- [ ] A new user can sign up, take an IELTS Reading Mini, see results — under 5 minutes total.
- [ ] At least 50 admin-curated AI-generated questions in the bank, all `status='approved'`.
- [ ] Pricing page live at `aiexam.uz/pricing`; paid checkout clearly marked "coming soon" until Phase 4.
- [ ] Privacy Policy + ToS published in UZ + EN.
- [ ] Error monitoring (Sentry) capturing FE + BE in production.
- [ ] DB backups configured and tested (restore from a 24h-old dump succeeds).
- [ ] All 4 demo accounts (admin, content_admin, examiner, student) work in production with seeded data.
- [ ] Domain has valid TLS, HSTS preload, no mixed content.
